I am a researcher (研究员) at the [School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University](https://cst.qd.sdu.edu.cn/), based in Qingdao, China. I joined Shandong University in November 2023.

Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at [Nanyang Technological University](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/), Singapore (2021&ndash;2023), working with [Prof. Thomas Peyrin](https://thomaspeyrin.github.io/web/). I received my Ph.D. (2021) from [Shandong University](https://www.sdu.edu.cn/), under the supervision of [Prof. Meiqin Wang](https://faculty.sdu.edu.cn/wangmeiqin1/zh_CN/index.htm).

## Research Interests

My research focuses on **symmetric-key cryptography**, in particular:

- Cryptanalysis of symmetric-key primitives (block ciphers, stream ciphers, AEAD, hash functions).
- Mathematical foundations and automated tools for cipher design and cryptanalysis (division property, cube attacks, differential/linear/integral cryptanalysis, MILP/SAT/SMT-based search).
- Connections between cryptanalysis and machine learning security.
