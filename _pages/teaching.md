**Cryptographic Design and Analysis Practice** (密码设计与分析实践)
Undergraduate course, School of Cyber Science and Technology, Shandong University. Offered every Spring semester.

This is a lab-based course that teaches the fundamental ideas and techniques of symmetric-key cryptanalysis and design through hands-on experiments. Students learn the theory by doing experiments.
